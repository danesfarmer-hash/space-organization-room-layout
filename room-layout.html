    Vertical measurements inches

    Edit either gap to move the object without changing its height. Floor gap + height + ceiling gap equals the room height.

    Free-position room feature. Soffit defaults overhead; Bump Out defaults to 0" AFF at the floor. Both use identical Width/Depth dimensions, room-location dimensions, corner resize, mid-edge X/Y resize, snapping, copy/paste, delete, and X/Y drawing axes. Click selects; click-and-drag the body moves the feature. Soffits and Bump Outs are constrained to the room footprint and cannot extend beyond the room boundary.
    Slope heights above floor

    Low and high points drive the slope automatically—no angle entry. Add independent sections and share high points to form an A-frame or define a separate back slope.

    Location measurements inches

    Measured to the near edge, not the center. Enter a distance or drag in the layout; the latest valid edit updates both. Fractions and math are supported. Use a space for mixed fractions; − means subtraction.

    Select the Item to show its room-location dimensions. Vent and Access Panel also show overall plan dimensions; Properties still control their Width/Height.
